What is a tense?

Secara sederhana, tense membantu kita menunjukkan bagaimana suatu keadaan atau kejadian ditempatkan dalam kaitannya dengan waktu. Tetapi saat memilih bentuk verb, kita tidak hanya bertanya kapan sesuatu terjadi. Kita juga perlu melihat bagaimana pembicara melihat aktivitasnya.

01 · TENSE 1

Present Simple

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Present Simple digunakan untuk membicarakan sesuatu yang dianggap sebagai keadaan, kebiasaan, rutinitas, atau fakta yang berlaku secara umum.

Oxford specifically describes the present simple as being used for present states, repeated actions/routines/habits, and permanent facts.

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The situation is presented as a usual, repeated, or generally true situation. It is not necessarily happening at this exact second.

Formula

Positive

I/You/We/They + V1

He/She/It + V1-s/-es

Examples:

I study Business Administration.

She studies English every day.

Negative

I/You/We/They + do not + V1

He/She/It + does not + V1

I don't drink coffee.

She doesn't drink coffee.

Question

Do + subject + V1?

Does + subject + V1?

Do you speak English?

Does she speak English?

Main uses

1. Habits and routines

I go to the gym three times a week.

She studies every evening.

2. Permanent or usual situations

I live in Istanbul.

He works for a technology company.

3. General facts

Water boils at 100°C.

The Earth revolves around the Sun.

Important note

Jangan mengajarkan:

❌ Present Simple = something happening now.

It can describe something that is true now, but its core idea is not necessarily “happening at this exact moment.”

Compare:

I work from home.

I am working from home today.

Yang pertama menggambarkan general/regular situation. Yang kedua menggambarkan temporary situation.

Oxford makes exactly this distinction between permanent routines and temporary activities.

Common time expressions

every day usually often always sometimes every week on Mondays once a year
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

02 · TENSE 2

Present Continuous

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Present Continuous digunakan untuk membicarakan aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung dalam suatu periode sekarang, atau situasi yang bersifat sementara.

Oxford describes it as an action that is continuing over a period of time and something that we are “in the middle of.”

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The activity is viewed as being in progress during a period around now. The period can be wider than the exact moment of speaking.

Formula

Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

I am studying.

She is working.

They are travelling.

Main uses

1. Action happening around now

I am studying for my exam.

She is talking to her professor.

2. Temporary situation

I am staying with my friend this week.

She is working in London this month.

3. Temporary repeated activity

I'm travelling to work by bus this week.

Oxford specifically contrasts a temporary series of actions with the permanent routine expressed by the present simple.

Compare

I work from home.

I am working from home this week.

work → normal/regular situation

am working → temporary situation

Common time expressions

now right now at the moment today this week this month currently these days
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

03 · TENSE 3

Present Perfect

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

This is one of the sections where I would really avoid oversimplifying for your website.

Don't teach:

❌ Present Perfect = past action that has a connection to the present.

That's not wrong, but it's too vague for beginners.

A better explanation:

Basic idea

Present Perfect digunakan ketika kita melihat suatu kejadian dari masa lalu sampai titik sekarang. Fokusnya bukan pada waktu lampau yang sudah selesai, tetapi pada hubungannya dengan sekarang.

Oxford describes it as an action in the period leading up to the present, and also uses it for repeated actions and states continuing up to now.

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

Look back from NOW. The earlier event is connected to the period up to the present or is relevant to the present.

Formula

Subject + have/has + V3

I have finished my homework.

She has visited Japan three times.

Main uses

1. Past action with a present result

I have lost my keys.

Artinya sekarang saya tidak punya/tidak menemukan kunci saya.

2. Experience up to now

I have visited Japan three times.

Have you ever been to Germany?

3. Situation/state continuing until now

I have lived here for three years.

She has known him since 2020.

Very important comparison

I lost my keys.

→ Kita melihat kejadian itu sebagai past event.

I have lost my keys.

→ Kejadian tersebut masih relevan sekarang; misalnya saya masih tidak punya kuncinya.

Oxford explicitly contrasts the present perfect with the past simple based on whether the speaker sees the action as connected to the present or as part of a finished past period.

Common time expressions

just already yet ever never recently so far for since this week
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

04 · TENSE 4

Present Perfect Continuous

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Present Perfect Continuous digunakan untuk melihat suatu aktivitas yang berlangsung selama suatu periode hingga sekarang. Fokusnya lebih kepada proses atau durasi aktivitas tersebut.

Oxford says it is used for an action over a period of time up to now.

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The activity extends toward NOW. The visual emphasis is on the activity or duration rather than only the result.

Formula

Subject + have/has + been + V-ing

I have been studying for three hours.

She has been working since morning.

Main uses

1. Activity continuing until now

I have been studying for three hours.

2. Activity that has recently stopped but has a present result

You look tired.

— I've been studying all night.

3. Repeated activity continuing up to now

I've been going to the gym regularly.

Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

This distinction is GOLD for your website.

I have written five pages.

Focus → result / number

I have been writing for three hours.

Focus → activity / duration

Oxford explicitly explains this distinction: the continuous focuses on the action/process, while the present perfect focuses more on the result; when we say how long, the continuous is normally used, while how many favors the non-continuous form.

Common time expressions

for since all day all morning lately recently how long this week
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

05 · TENSE 5

Past Simple

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Past Simple digunakan untuk membicarakan sesuatu yang terjadi atau berlangsung di masa lalu dan kita melihatnya sebagai bagian dari masa lalu.

Oxford describes it as the normal tense for an action in the past, especially when the relevant time is finished.

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The event is placed in a finished PAST period and is presented as a past event.

Formula

Positive:

Subject + V2

I visited Paris last year.

She studied in Germany.

Negative:

Subject + did not + V1

I didn't visit Paris.

Question:

Did + subject + V1?

Did you visit Paris?

Examples

I graduated from high school in 2021.

She went to Germany last year.

We watched a movie yesterday.

Important

Setelah did/didn't, gunakan V1, bukan V2.

❌ Did you went?

✅ Did you go?

Oxford explicitly gives this rule.

Common time expressions

yesterday last week last year in 2020 two days ago then when I was younger
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

06 · TENSE 6

Past Continuous

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Past Continuous digunakan untuk melihat suatu aktivitas sebagai sesuatu yang sedang berlangsung pada suatu titik atau periode di masa lalu.

Oxford describes it as an action over a period of past time — something that we were in the middle of.

VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The activity is in progress at a point or during a period in the PAST. Another Past Simple event may happen during it.

Formula

Subject + was/were + V-ing

I was studying.

They were travelling.

Examples

I was studying at 10 p.m.

She was cooking when I called her.

Past Continuous + Past Simple

Ini salah satu konsep terpenting:

I was taking a shower when the phone rang.

was taking → longer/background action

rang → shorter event

Oxford explicitly describes the continuous action as the longer/background action and the past simple as the shorter action that interrupts it.

Common time expressions

at 8 p.m. at that time when while all evening all night this time yesterday
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

07 · TENSE 7

Past Perfect

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Past Perfect digunakan ketika kita melihat suatu kejadian dari titik waktu di masa lalu dan ingin menunjukkan bahwa kejadian lain sudah terjadi lebih dahulu.
VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

Look back from a later PAST point. The Past Perfect event happened before that reference point.

Formula:

Subject + had + V3

I had finished my homework before I went out.

Timeline:

finish homework → go out → past

Example

When I arrived, the movie had already started.

Artinya:

movie started → I arrived

Oxford defines the past perfect as an action before a past time.

And this is a very useful example from Oxford:

When the boss arrived, the meeting began.

vs.

When the boss arrived, the meeting had begun.

Yang kedua berarti the meeting began before the boss arrived.

Common time expressions

before after by the time already just until then previously
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

08 · TENSE 8

Past Perfect Continuous

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Past Perfect Continuous digunakan untuk melihat aktivitas yang berlangsung selama suatu periode sebelum titik tertentu di masa lalu. Fokusnya pada proses atau durasi.
VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The activity continued for a period before a PAST reference point. Focus on the process or duration.

Formula:

Subject + had been + V-ing

I had been studying for three hours when she called.

Example

She was tired because she had been working all day.

The activity:

working → working → working → past point

Compare

I had cleaned the room.

Focus → result/completed action.

I had been cleaning the room.

Focus → activity/process.

Oxford makes exactly this distinction: past perfect continuous focuses on the action going on, while past perfect can focus on the completed result.

Common time expressions

for since all day all morning before until then for hours
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

09 · TENSE 9

Future Simple — WILL

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Will digunakan untuk membicarakan masa depan, terutama ketika kita membuat prediction, membuat keputusan secara spontan, atau menawarkan/menjanjikan sesuatu.
VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

The reference point is in the FUTURE. Will commonly expresses prediction, instant decision, offer, promise, or willingness.

Formula:

Subject + will + V1

I will call you tomorrow.

She will probably come.

Oxford says will + base form expresses the future and particularly discusses predictions, instant decisions, willingness, offers and promises.

Instant decision

The phone is ringing.

I'll answer it.

Keputusan dibuat saat berbicara.

Compare:

I'm going to answer it.

This suggests the intention/decision already existed.

Common time expressions

tomorrow next week next year soon later in the future I think probably
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

10 · TENSE 10

Future Continuous

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Future Continuous digunakan untuk membicarakan aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada suatu titik atau periode di masa depan.
VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

At the FUTURE reference point, the activity will already be in progress.

Formula:

Subject + will be + V-ing

I will be studying at 8 p.m. tomorrow.

Example

This time tomorrow, I'll be flying to London.

At that future moment, the activity will already be in progress.

Oxford describes this as an action over a period of future time — we will be in the middle of an action.

Compare:

I will study at 8 p.m.

→ future action.

I will be studying at 8 p.m.

→ at 8 p.m., the activity will be in progress.

Common time expressions

this time tomorrow at 8 p.m. tomorrow all day all evening next week at this time when you arrive
Do not choose the tense from a keyword alone.

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Read the whole sentence, identify the reference point, and decide what the speaker wants to emphasize.

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the form. Finally, prove that you can choose it.

Basic idea

Future Perfect digunakan untuk membicarakan sesuatu yang akan sudah selesai sebelum atau pada titik tertentu di masa depan.
VISUAL TIMELINE See where the action sits.
PAST NOW FUTURE

Look back from a FUTURE reference point. The action will already be over by that point.

Formula:

Subject + will have + V3

I will have finished the report by Friday.

Timeline:

NOW → finish report → FRIDAY

Examples

By next year, I will have graduated from university.

She will have finished the project by Monday.

Oxford defines this as looking back from the future and talking about something that will be over at a future time.

Future Perfect Continuous digunakan untuk membicarakan aktivitas yang akan sudah berlangsung selama suatu durasi sampai titik tertentu di masa depan.

By next month, I will have been working here for two years.

duration + ongoing activity

THE 5-STEP TENSE METHOD

01 WHEN?

Find the time.

Apakah situasinya berada di Present, Past, atau Future?

02 REFERENCE POINT

Find the point you are looking from.

Untuk perfect forms, tentukan titik waktu yang menjadi tempat kamu “melihat kembali”.

03 HOW?

Choose the aspect.

Simple, Continuous, Perfect, atau Perfect Continuous?

04 CONTEXT

Read the whole sentence.

Jangan hanya mencari satu keyword. Lihat hubungan antarkejadian dan maksud pembicara.

05 · CHECK THE MEANING

Tanyakan: apakah kalimat ini benar-benar menyampaikan makna yang saya inginkan? Kalau dua tense sama-sama mungkin, pilih berdasarkan bagaimana kamu ingin melihat situasinya.
