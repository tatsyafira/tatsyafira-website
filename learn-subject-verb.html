01

Foundation

WHAT IS SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT?

Understand the basic idea.

Locked

Subject dan Verb harus "match"

Dalam sebuah kalimat, subject adalah orang, benda, atau hal yang melakukan suatu tindakan atau yang sedang dibicarakan.

Verb adalah kata yang menunjukkan tindakan, keadaan, atau kondisi dari subject.

Contoh:

“

The student studies every night.

Tanyakan:

“

Who studies every night?

Jawabannya:

“

The student

Jadi:

  • The student = subject
  • studies = verb

Karena student adalah singular, verb-nya juga harus mengikuti bentuk singular:

“

The student studies.

Sekarang ubah subject-nya menjadi plural:

“

The students study every night.

Sekarang:

  • The students = plural subject
  • study = plural verb

Perhatikan bahwa bentuk verb justru berubah dari:

“

studies → study

Basic Pattern

Untuk sebagian besar verbs dalam simple present:

Singular
“

He works.

She studies.

The company grows.

Plural
“

They work.

The students study.

The companies grow.

Cara paling mudah untuk mengingatnya:

“

He / She / It → Verb + S

“

I / You / We / They → Base Verb

Tapi jangan langsung menghafalkan "singular = verb + S"

Ini hanya berlaku untuk pola tertentu, terutama simple present.

Misalnya:

“

She works.

Tetapi:

“

She is working.

Di sini verb utamanya adalah working, sedangkan agreement ditunjukkan oleh auxiliary is.

Atau:

“

She can work.

Bukan:

“

❌ She can works.

Jadi, kita tidak bisa memahami subject–verb agreement hanya dengan satu aturan "singular tambah S".

Kita perlu melihat jenis verb dan struktur kalimatnya.

“

student = subject

“

“

Jawab:

“

The company

Jadi:

“

company = subject

“

“

The students

SUBJECT ≠ THE NEAREST NOUN

Ini adalah salah satu kesalahan paling umum dalam subject–verb agreement.

Perhatikan:

“

The student in the classroom studies every day.

Ada beberapa nouns di dalam kalimat, tetapi bukan berarti semuanya bisa menjadi subject.

Di sini:

“

The student = subject

classroom = noun di dalam prepositional phrase

Tanyakan:

“

Who studies every day?

Bukan:

“

classroom

Tetapi:

“

the student

Maka:

“

The student studies every day.

The "Remove the Middle" Technique

Ketika sebuah kalimat terlihat panjang dan membingungkan, hapus sementara informasi tambahan di antara subject dan verb.

Contoh:

“

The student in the business administration department studies every evening.

Hapus bagian:

“

in the business administration department

Maka:

“

The student studies every evening.

Sekarang subject dan verb terlihat jelas.

Example
“

The quality of the products is excellent.

Hapus:

“

of the products

Maka:

“

The quality is excellent.

Jadi:

“

quality → is

Bukan:

“

products → are

Another example
“

The effects of social media on young people are significant.

Hapus informasi tambahannya:

“

The effects are significant.

Jadi:

“

effects → are

Important Rule

“

### Jangan memilih verb berdasarkan noun yang paling dekat dengan verb.

Temukan real subject terlebih dahulu.

HEAD NOUN

Dalam sebuah subject yang panjang, kita perlu menemukan head noun.

Head noun adalah noun utama yang menjadi inti dari sebuah noun phrase.

Contoh:

“

The quality of the products

Keseluruhan phrase:

“

The quality of the products

Tetapi head noun-nya adalah:

“

quality

Maka:

“

The quality of the products is excellent.

Contoh lain:

“

The effects of social media

Head noun:

“

effects

Maka:

“

The effects of social media are significant.

Contoh:

“

The number of international students

Head noun:

“

number

Maka:

“

The number of international students is increasing.

Cara menemukan Head Noun

Tanyakan:

“

"Apa noun utama dalam subject ini?"

Contoh:

“

The rapid development of artificial intelligence

Noun yang menjadi pusat phrase tersebut adalah:

“

development

Bukan:

“

intelligence

Jadi:

“

The rapid development of artificial intelligence has created new opportunities.

06

Patterns

COMMON AGREEMENT PATTERNS

Recognize common agreement patterns.

Locked

Sekarang kita masuk ke pola-pola yang paling sering muncul dalam soal grammar.

A. AND → USUALLY PLURAL

Jika dua subject yang berbeda dihubungkan dengan and, biasanya verb-nya plural.

“

Tom and Sarah are students.

“

The manager and the employee are discussing the problem.

“

Supply chain and logistics are important fields.

Logikanya:

“

A + B = two subjects

Maka:

“

plural verb

Tetapi ada pengecualian

Kadang dua nouns merujuk pada satu orang atau satu combined concept.

“

The CEO and founder is speaking.

Jika CEO dan founder adalah orang yang sama, subject-nya satu orang.

Bandingkan:

“

The CEO and the founder are speaking.

Ini biasanya berarti ada dua orang berbeda.

Jadi, tanyakan:

“

Are these two separate people/things, or one person/combined idea?

B. EACH → SINGULAR

Each memperlakukan anggota kelompok satu per satu.

Maka:

“

Each + singular verb

Examples:

“

Each student has a textbook.

“

Each employee receives training.

“

Each participant is required to register.

Bukan:

“

❌ Each student have a textbook.

C. EVERY → SINGULAR

Every juga diikuti singular noun dan singular verb.

“

Every student needs an ID card.

“

Every employee has a responsibility.

“

Every participant is required to register.

Bukan:

“

❌ Every student need an ID card.

D. EVERYONE / EVERYBODY → SINGULAR

Ini sangat penting.

Walaupun everyone berarti banyak orang, secara grammar ia dianggap singular.

“

Everyone is ready.

“

Everybody has a ticket.

“

Someone knows the answer.

“

Nobody wants to leave.

Bukan:

“

❌ Everyone are ready.

Words like these are singular:

“

everyone

everybody

someone

somebody

anyone

anybody

no one

nobody

Cara mengingatnya:

“

-one / -body → singular

E. ONE OF + PLURAL NOUN → SINGULAR

Perhatikan:

“

One of the students is absent.

Bukan:

“

❌ One of the students are absent.

Kenapa?

Subject-nya adalah:

“

one

Bukan:

“

students

Kalau kita hapus bagian tambahan:

“

One of the students is absent.

Maka jelas:

“

one → singular → is

Examples:

“

One of the reasons is the high cost.

“

One of my friends has moved abroad.

“

One of the best universities is located in Istanbul.

The OF trap

THE "OF" TRAP

Stop choosing the nearest noun.

Locked

Ini adalah salah satu bagian paling penting untuk TOEFL.

Banyak learner melihat:

“

noun + of + noun

lalu memilih verb berdasarkan noun setelah of.

Padahal, itu sering salah.

THE MAIN RULE

“

### The noun after "of" is not automatically the subject.

Perhatikan:

“

The quality of the products is excellent.

Subject:

“

quality

Bukan:

“

products

A. THE NUMBER OF

“

The number of + plural noun → singular verb

Example:

“

The number of students is increasing.

Kenapa?

Karena head noun-nya adalah:

“

number

Students hanya menjelaskan apa yang sedang dihitung.

B. A NUMBER OF

Berbeda dengan sebelumnya:

“

A number of + plural noun → plural verb

Example:

“

A number of students are absent.

Di sini:

“

a number of = many / several

Maka:

“

students → plural → are

Compare

“

The number of students is increasing.

= total number

“

A number of students are absent.

= many students

Hafalkan pasangan ini:
“

The number of → IS

“

A number of → ARE

C. EACH OF

“

Each of the students has a laptop.

Walaupun students plural, subject-nya adalah:

“

each

Maka:

“

has

D. ONE OF

“

One of the students is absent.

Subject:

“

one

Maka:

“

is

E. QUALITY OF

“

The quality of the products is excellent.

Head noun:

“

quality

Maka:

“

is

Bandingkan:

“

The products of the company are excellent.

Sekarang head noun-nya:

“

products

Maka:

“

are

Contohnya:

“

some

all

most

half

none

percentages

fractions

Dalam banyak struktur ini, gunakan prinsip:

“

Look at the noun after "of."

A. SOME OF

Uncountable noun → singular
“

Some of the money is missing.

“

Some of the information is incorrect.

Plural noun → plural
“

Some of the students are absent.

“

Some of the books are missing.

B. ALL OF

“

All of the information is useful.

“

All of the money has been spent.

Tetapi:

“

All of the students are present.

“

All of the books have been sold.

C. HALF OF

“

Tetapi:

“

Half of the students are absent.

“

“

“

“

Most of the money has been spent.

“

Most of the books have been sold.

Special cases

SPECIAL CASES

Handle exceptions and tricky structures.

Locked

A. THERE IS / THERE ARE

Dalam struktur there is / there are, jangan menganggap there sebagai subject.

Real subject berada setelah verb.

Singular
“

There is a problem.

Real subject:

“

a problem

Plural
“

There are several problems.

Real subject:

“

several problems

Compare
“

There is a student in the classroom.

“

There are ten students in the classroom.

Jadi ketika melihat:

“

There is / There are

langsung tanyakan:

“

What comes after the verb?

B. HERE IS / HERE ARE

Prinsipnya sama.

“

Here is the report.

“

Here are the reports.

“

Here is the information you requested.

“

Here are the documents you requested.

C. RELATIVE CLAUSES: WHO / WHICH / THAT

Relative clauses dapat membuat subject–verb agreement menjadi lebih sulit.

Contoh:

“

The student who works here is my friend.

Apa yang dirujuk oleh who?

“

student

Karena student singular:

“

works

Bandingkan:

“

The students who work here are my friends.

Sekarang who merujuk kepada:

“

students

Karena students plural:

“

work

The key question

Jika menemukan:

“

who / which / that

tanyakan:

“

What noun does it refer to?

Setelah tahu noun-nya, tentukan:

“

singular atau plural?

Kemudian pilih verb yang sesuai.

D. COLLECTIVE NOUNS

Collective nouns adalah nouns yang merujuk kepada sebuah kelompok.

Contohnya:

“

team

family

committee

company

organization

government

audience

class

staff

Dalam American English, collective nouns biasanya menggunakan singular verb ketika kelompok tersebut dianggap sebagai satu unit.

“

The team is winning.

“

The committee has reached a decision.

“

The company is growing.

“

The government has announced a new policy.

British English kadang menggunakan plural verb ketika yang ditekankan adalah individual members.

Namun untuk academic English dan standardized tests, singular agreement merupakan pilihan yang aman ketika kelompok tersebut bertindak sebagai satu unit.

E. MEASUREMENTS

Expressions yang menunjukkan waktu, uang, jarak, atau measurement dapat menggunakan singular verb ketika dianggap sebagai satu total amount.

“

Ten dollars is enough.

“

Five years is a long time.

“

Two hours is enough to finish the exam.

“

Ten kilometers is too far to walk.

Dalam contoh tersebut, kita melihat jumlah tersebut sebagai satu total amount.

F. WORDS ENDING IN -S

Jangan menganggap:

“

word ending in -s = plural

Tidak selalu.

Contohnya:

“

Economics is important.

“

Mathematics is difficult.

“

Statistics is an important field.

“

The news is surprising.

“

Politics is complicated.

STATISTICS

Kata statistics bisa memiliki fungsi yang berbeda.

Sebagai bidang ilmu:

“

Statistics is an important subject.

Sebagai numerical data:

“

The statistics are concerning.

Jadi selalu lihat meaning dan function dalam kalimat.

G. GERUNDS AS SUBJECTS

Bentuk -ing dapat berfungsi sebagai noun.

Contoh:

“

Studying is important.

“

Exercising is beneficial.

“

Learning English takes time.

Di sini, kegiatan tersebut dianggap sebagai satu idea.

Maka:

“

Studying is

Bukan:

“

❌ Studying are

H. INFINITIVES AS SUBJECTS

Infinitive juga dapat berfungsi sebagai subject.

“

To learn a language takes time.

“

To travel abroad is expensive.

“

To exercise regularly requires discipline.

Keseluruhan activity dianggap sebagai satu idea.

THE 5-STEP EXAM METHOD

Use the five-step system.

Locked

Sekarang kita ubah semua rules di atas menjadi satu sistem yang bisa kamu gunakan saat mengerjakan soal.

STEP 1 — FIND

Cari verb yang sedang diuji.

Contoh:

“

The quality of the products has / have improved.

Verb:

“

has / have

STEP 2 — ASK

Tanyakan:

“

Who or what has improved?

Jawaban:

“

The quality

STEP 3 — REMOVE

Hapus sementara informasi yang mengganggu.

“

The quality of the products has improved.

STEP 4 — IDENTIFY

Tentukan apakah subject-nya singular atau plural.

“

quality = singular

STEP 5 — MATCH

Karena subject singular:

“

has

Maka:

“

The quality of the products has improved.

THE FAST VERSION

Kalau sedang mengerjakan TOEFL dan waktunya terbatas, gunakan:

“

## FIND → REMOVE → IDENTIFY → CHECK → MATCH

FIND

Cari verb.

↓

REMOVE

Abaikan distracting phrases.

↓

IDENTIFY

Cari real subject/head noun.

↓

CHECK

Singular atau plural?

↓

MATCH

Pilih verb yang sesuai.

TOEFL-STYLE EXAMPLE

“

The effects of social media on young people's mental health ___ becoming increasingly significant.

A. is B. are

Step 1 — FIND
“

is / are becoming

Step 2 — ASK
“

What is becoming significant?

“

The effects

Step 3 — REMOVE
“

The effects of social media on young people's mental health are becoming significant.

Step 4 — CHECK
“

effects = plural

Step 5 — MATCH
“

plural → are

Answer:
“

The effects of social media on young people's mental health are becoming increasingly significant.
